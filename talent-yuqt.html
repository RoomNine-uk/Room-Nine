DJ / Production

Y U QT

Y U QT are the UK DJ and production duo of lifelong friends Darryl Reid and James Cooper, two of the most exciting names to emerge from the new generation of UK garage and electronic music.

Born and raised in Leicestershire, the pair first met in Year 7 at Lutterworth High School, bonding over music, skateboarding and a shared obsession with club culture. After spending more than a decade pursuing separate paths, they reconnected and began making music together, officially emerging as Y U QT in 2019. Even the name came by chance, inspired by UY Scuti, one of the largest known stars, which Darryl stumbled across before Cooper flipped the letters into Y U QT.

Their sound moves freely between UK garage, house, bassline, jungle and rave, pairing the nostalgia of classic UK dance music with a distinctly modern energy. Their famously energetic sets are built largely from their own productions, edits and unreleased music, making their shows instantly recognisable.

Since breaking through, Y U QT have taken their sound worldwide, with sold out tours across the UK, Australia, New Zealand and Asia, alongside regular touring throughout Europe and Asia, including shows across Tokyo, Seoul, Singapore and beyond. They have appeared at Glastonbury, Parklife, Field Day, Pitch Festival, Waterworks, The Warehouse Project and Boiler Room, building a reputation as much for the energy behind the decks as the music itself.

Now signed to Technicolour / Ninja Tune, with support from BBC Radio 1, Rinse FM, Triple J and Apple Music’s Hyperhouse, Y U QT continue to sit at the forefront of UK dance music’s global resurgence.
