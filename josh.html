Model / Athlete

Josh

Josh is a London-based model, athlete and fighter with a distinctive look and a growing international portfolio. At 19, he has modelled for brands including Footasylum, Reebok, Stone Island, New Balance, Mertra and Syna World, alongside work with brands in Brazil, Spain and Italy.

Comfortable in the gym and across fashion, fitness, calisthenics, martial arts, lifestyle and acting, he adapts to each shoot. Alongside modelling, he has built an active audience of 30K total followers across TikTok and Instagram, with 1.6M likes on TikTok.
