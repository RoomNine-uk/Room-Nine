LostBoys

Nottingham-based creative collective made up of Fez, Eddz, Mullz, Luke, S5, Ronin, Chef Dee and Marz. Built around fashion, music, events and content, the group has grown into a cultural movement with its own brand, community and highly recognisable identity. They have supported Dave on tour and worked with Nike, NOCTA, Adidas, Mizuno, One Block Down, C.P. Company, Jordan and Wingstop.
