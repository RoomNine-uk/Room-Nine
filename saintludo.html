Saint Ludo

Saint Ludo is a London-based DJ and producer bringing a high-energy, vocal-led approach to UK bass, moving through UKG, jungle, grime and rap-influenced club music. A Rinse FM resident, she has played Glastonbury and Reading & Leeds, shared stages with Nia Archives and Overmono, and played US dates with Sammy Virji. Her track “Subliminals” featuring Kasst 8 has received support across BBC Radio 1, 1Xtra and Apple Music 1, alongside a Skrillex co-sign. Her debut EP is upcoming.
